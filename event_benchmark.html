Strengths:

Rolling splits advance one week at a time: selected training weeks, then one calibration week and one test week. They can take substantially longer; short or missing data may leave detectors or injections unsupported.

Cross-household runs compare only Isolation Forests on the same injected events. The shared model uses training households only; the personal baseline uses the target household's earlier history. Use development households to choose settings and reserve final test households for evaluation. Corrected v2 is required. Daily rolling windows cannot calibrate the personal Isolation Forest: one week has fewer than 10 observations.
